Good to know

Before the first whistle.

Do you offer team training and private lessons?

Yes. Team training builds individual skills and team play. Private lessons provide one-on-one coaching focused on the player’s goals. Prices and availability will be published before booking opens.

Does my child need soccer experience?

The proposed First Touches pathway is designed for new players. Final eligibility and age groups will be confirmed before registration opens.

Where and when will sessions take place?

Training takes place at Lawrence Park in Indianapolis, Indiana. Session dates, times, and the exact meeting point will be shared before families can register.

What should a player bring?

Every player must bring their own soccer ball to team training and private lessons. Also bring water, comfortable sportswear, shin guards, and shoes appropriate for the playing surface.

How much does training cost?

Fees and any financial assistance options have not been announced. Confirmed details will be listed with each program.

Can I register now?

Registration is not open yet. The training planner above is a guide only; it does not submit an application or book a session.
