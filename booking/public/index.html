Good to know

Before the first whistle.

Do you offer team training and private lessons?

Yes. Private lessons cost $50 for one hour. Group training costs $20 per person for one hour and requires at least 11 players booked together.

Does my child need soccer experience?

The proposed First Touches pathway is designed for new players. Final eligibility and age groups will be confirmed before registration opens.

Where and when will sessions take place?

Training takes place at Lawrence Park in Indianapolis, Indiana. Hours are Monday–Friday 6–8 PM, Saturday 2–6 PM, and Sunday 2–5 PM. Each session lasts one hour.

What should a player bring?

Every player must bring their own soccer ball to team training and private lessons. Also bring water, comfortable sportswear, shin guards, and shoes appropriate for the playing surface.

How much does training cost?

Private lessons are $50 per hour for one player. Group lessons are $20 per person for one hour, with a minimum of 11 players. A group of 11 totals $220.

Can I register now?

Use the booking page to check whether online reservations are open. It will display a confirmation only after your hour has been reserved.
